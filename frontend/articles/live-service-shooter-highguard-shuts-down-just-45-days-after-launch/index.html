Wildlight Entertainment, founded by former 'Apex Legends' developers, has announced that its live service raid shooter 'Highguard' will permanently shut down on March 12, 2026.

Launched on January 26, 2026, the game will have lasted an exceptionally brief 45 days before its closure. The announcement was shared via the game's official X (formerly Twitter) account, with a statement from the studio beginning, "Today we're sharing difficult news. We have made the decision to permanently shut down Highguard on March 12."

While the game industry has seen a continuous influx of live service titles, cases of games failing to meet initial expectations and shutting down quickly are not uncommon. The 'Highguard' case highlights the significant challenges involved in sustaining new IPs long-term in an increasingly competitive market.
