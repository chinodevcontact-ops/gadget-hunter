MSI's new AM5 motherboard, the "MAG X870 Tomahawk WiFi," has been spotted on Amazon for $240 (approx. ¥37,680).

This motherboard features AMD's next-generation X870 chipset and comes equipped with the latest WiFi 7 wireless standard, high-speed USB4 ports, and dual Gen5 M.2 slots for next-gen SSDs. These specifications enable users to maximize the performance of AMD Ryzen 7000/8000/9000 series processors.

The availability of a feature-rich X870 motherboard at this price point presents an attractive option for users considering upgrading to or building an AM5 system. It is expected to draw significant attention from PC builders seeking high performance and future-proofing capabilities.
