Nintendo has announced that the new battling game, 'Pokémon Champions,' is set to release in April 2026 for both the Nintendo Switch and the upcoming Nintendo Switch 2. A specific launch date is expected to be revealed at a later time. Concurrently, mobile versions for iOS and Android are also under development, signaling a broad multi-platform strategy.

First showcased at Pokémon Presents events in July 2025 and February 2026, the game emphasizes Pokémon battling. A recent overview trailer, debuted via the Nintendo Today! app, provided detailed insights into its core gameplay elements. This extensive platform rollout suggests Nintendo's aim to reach a wide audience, encompassing current and next-gen console owners, as well as mobile gamers.
