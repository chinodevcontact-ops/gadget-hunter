現在、DRAM、NANDフラッシュ、グラフィックボードといった主要PCパーツの価格が高騰傾向にある中、このような大幅な割引は極めて異例な事例と言えます。大手小売店では稀にこうした価格設定の誤りやプロモーションが発生することがありますが、消費者がその恩恵にあずかる機会は非常に低いのが現状です。

A Reddit user has reported acquiring a high-performance gaming PC at an astonishing price from a Costco store in the US.

The iBuyPower system reportedly features AMD's next-gen high-end CPU, the Ryzen 9800X3D, NVIDIA's forthcoming mid-range GPU, the GeForce RTX 5070, alongside 32GB of DDR5 RAM and a 2TB NVMe SSD. While this configuration is estimated to have a market value of approximately $2,500, the user reportedly purchased it for just $1,000.

This significant discount is particularly noteworthy given the current trend of rising prices for key PC components such as DRAM, NAND flash, and graphics cards. While such pricing anomalies or promotional offers occasionally occur at major retailers, the chances of consumers encountering them remain exceptionally low.
