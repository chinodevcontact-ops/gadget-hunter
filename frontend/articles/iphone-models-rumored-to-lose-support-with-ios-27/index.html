Information regarding the supported models for iOS 27 has been released by Instant Digital, an Apple information leaker active on the Chinese social media platform Weibo. According to this information, iOS 27 is expected to be compatible with the iPhone 12 series and newer.

On the other hand, the iPhone 11, iPhone 11 Pro, iPhone 11 Pro Max, and iPhone SE (2nd generation) may not be supported by iOS 27. These devices are expected to continue receiving iOS 26 security updates for at least the next few years.

The iPhone models that will support iOS 27 are: iPhone 17e, iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 16e, iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Plus, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max, iPhone 15, iPhone 15 Plus, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 14, iPhone 14 Plus, iPhone 14 Pro, iPhone 14 Pro Max, iPhone 13, iPhone 13 mini, iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 13 Pro Max, iPhone 12, iPhone 12 mini, iPhone 12 Pro, iPhone 12 Pro Max, and iPhone SE (3rd generation).

Additionally, new Apple Intelligence features introduced in iOS 27 will only be available on iPhone 15 Pro and newer models.

Apple is scheduled to announce iOS 27 at the WWDC 2026 keynote on June 8, 2026, and the first developer beta is expected to be released later that day. A public beta is anticipated around July, and the final release to all users is expected in September. iOS 27 is reportedly focused on bug fixes and stability improvements, similar to Mac OS X Snow Leopard. Expected new features include a dedicated Siri app, a system-wide slider for fine-tuning the opacity of Liquid Glass, and improved keyboard autocorrection.

Instant Digital has a track record of accurately leaking Apple information, such as the yellow color for the iPhone 14 and iPhone 14 Plus, and the Titanium Milanese Loop for the Apple Watch Ultra 2. However, the source's accuracy is not always guaranteed.
