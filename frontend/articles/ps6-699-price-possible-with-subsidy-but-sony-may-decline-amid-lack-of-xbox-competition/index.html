New leak information has emerged regarding the pricing of the next-generation PlayStation 6. According to reliable AMD leaker KeplerL2, the Bill of Materials (BOM) for the PS6 is currently estimated to be around $760.

However, a retail price of $699 is technically possible if Sony were to apply a 'reasonable subsidy' to keep the price as low as possible. Conversely, given the recent price increases for the PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 5 Pro, there's speculation that the PS6's retail price could approach $1,000.

KeplerL2 further suggests that Sony may have less incentive to aggressively subsidize the console's price, as they reportedly no longer view Xbox as direct competition, potentially influencing the final pricing strategy.
