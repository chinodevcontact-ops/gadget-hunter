Bungie has released details about the new 'Runner' class in their upcoming FPS, 'Marathon,' through a developer insights video. Runners utilize biosynthetic bodies with transferred consciousness for survival on Tau Ceti. Each shell features a different gameplay archetype, such as intel gathering, combat disruption, and combat healing, allowing players to assess enemy threats and strategize based on silhouettes. Furthermore, players can finely customize their shell's playstyle by combining cores, implants, weapons, and mods. Future updates will detail visual customization options through unlockable cosmetics via the Codex and other methods. This high level of customization is expected to provide players with diverse strategies and immersive gameplay.
